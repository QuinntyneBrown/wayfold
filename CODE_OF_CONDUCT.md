# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults or derogatory comments
- Publishing other people's private information without permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the repository owner. All reports are reviewed and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow this code.
