# Wayfold Business Plan

October 15, 2026 · Quinn

## Executive summary

Run by one founder working with AI, Wayfold has its first profitable month in August 2028 and earns $174K on $476K of revenue in 2029. Losses of $96K in 2027 and $17K in 2028 come first. $225K of starting capital covers the $130K low point in July 2028, and still covers it if members come in 30% below plan. All figures are in Canadian dollars; Year 1 is calendar 2027.

Wayfold is a trusted micro-community for people in Toronto and the GTA who buy, sell and look after cars. Members show their garage, list private sales, recommend the dealers, mechanics and products they use, and can show their home church as a trust signal. Dealers list full inventory, providers keep verified profiles, and every vehicle carries a price rating with public answers from verified financing professionals.

Nobody on Wayfold pays: not members, dealers, mechanics or financing professionals. Revenue comes from outside companies that want to reach GTA car buyers and owners:

- Referral fees from partner lenders, paid per funded car loan (38% of 2029 revenue)
- Insurance-quote and extended-warranty referrals (24%)
- Clearly labelled advertising (20%)
- Commissions on products members buy through their recommendations (18%)

Like Kijiji or a Reddit forum, Wayfold is built once and then kept stable. After launch, changes are limited to fixes, security and legal requirements. That keeps costs to one founder, AI tools and hosting, and every revenue stream grows with one number: members, who reach 45,000 by December 2029.

## Problem and solution

Buying, selling and servicing a car in the GTA is a trust problem, and today's marketplaces solve for reach, not trust. Ontario's regulator mediated about 5,400 complaints against dealers from 2016 to 2020, and about half ended with no resolution for the consumer ([Auditor General of Ontario, 2023](https://www.auditor.on.ca/en/content/annualreports/arreports/en23/1-12FU_OMVIC_en23.pdf)).

| Pain today | Who feels it | Wayfold's answer |
|---|---|---|
| Anonymous sellers and scam listings on classifieds | Private buyers and sellers | Member profiles with a garage, history of posts and an optional home church |
| No way to tell if a price or financing offer is fair | Buyers | Automatic price rating from comparable listings, plus public answers from verified financing professionals |
| Finding an honest mechanic or body shop relies on word of mouth | Car owners | Recommendations from members you can see and trust, attached to provider profiles |
| Dealer leads are expensive and low-intent | Dealers and providers | A local, high-trust audience; inventory with full detail and searchable next to private listings |

Wayfold is autos-only and GTA-only by design. That focus keeps listings relevant and lets reputation travel: a recommendation from someone in your community carries more weight than an anonymous star rating.

## Product and revenue model

Everyone uses Wayfold free. Outside companies pay to reach its members, and every paid link or ad says so where it appears. Keeping everyone free protects the community that makes Wayfold worth advertising on.

### What each user gets, free

- **Members:** profile with optional home church, a garage of owned cars, private-sale listings, recommendations, posts, and questions to financing professionals.
- **Dealers:** a profile and full inventory with year, make, model, price, photos and description; every vehicle has its own searchable page with a price rating.
- **Providers** (mechanics, body shops, tire shops, detailers, towing, insurance brokers, parts stores): a profile that collects member recommendations.
- **Financing professionals:** a verified profile and the right to answer members' public deal questions.

### The product stays the same

Wayfold launches with every feature above and then holds still, like Google's search page, Reddit or Kijiji. Members learn it once, search engines index stable pages, and one person can run it. After launch the only changes are bug fixes, security updates, legal requirements and repairs to partner integrations.

### Who pays

| Stream | Who pays | What triggers a payment | Model assumption |
|---|---|---|---|
| Financing referrals | Partner banks, credit unions and auto lenders | A member applies through Wayfold and the loan is funded | $300 per funded loan; 12% of members buy a vehicle each year and 15% of buyers finance with a partner |
| Insurance and warranty referrals | Insurers, quote-comparison services and warranty companies | A completed insurance quote or a warranty sold | $25 per quote from 10% of members a year; $150 per warranty from 5% of buyers |
| Advertising | Auto brands, tire and parts makers, GTA businesses | Labelled ads shown on search and vehicle pages | 30 pageviews per member a month at $8 per 1,000 pageviews |
| Product commissions | Online retailers | A member buys a recommended product through Wayfold's link | $2.50 per member a year |

Partners are outside companies, not Wayfold profiles. A dealer, broker or shop with a profile stays free whether or not its company is also a partner, and price ratings and search order never depend on who pays.

Open question: every payout rate above is an assumption to confirm in the first partner agreements.

## Go-to-market

Wayfold grows through churches and search, not a sales team. Members come in through church groups, and dealers and providers join because listing is free and members are already there.

1. **Seed members through churches (from January 2027).** The founder partners with 15 GTA churches in 2027, 40 by the end of 2028 and 70 by the end of 2029. Each partner gets a short talk, a car-care workshop or a free vehicle-inspection day run with a member mechanic.
2. **Fill the site with inventory (January to March 2027).** The founder invites church-connected dealers first; dealers then sign up and list inventory themselves. Private sellers list free alongside them.
3. **Let recommendations bring in providers.** Every member recommendation creates or enriches a free provider profile, and the founder invites the most-recommended providers to complete theirs.
4. **Switch on partners and ads (April 2027).** Sign two or three lenders, an insurance-quote partner, a warranty partner, an ad network and retail affiliate programs.
5. **Let search do the rest.** Every vehicle page and public deal answer is a stable page that search engines index. From 2028, search is the main source of new members.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Search (vehicle pages, deal answers) | Members | Launch | Main source of new members | Main source of new members |

The budgets are the full marketing line in the financial projection, including events and printed material.

## Operations: one founder and AI

One founder runs Wayfold. AI does the routine work, and the founder makes every decision that touches trust, money or the law. A product that stays the same is what makes this possible: with no feature roadmap, there is no engineering team to hire.

### Who does what

| Job | AI does | Founder does |
|---|---|---|
| Code and maintenance | Writes fixes, security updates and tests | Reviews and ships every change |
| Moderation | Screens listings, posts and recommendations for scams, spam and rule breaks | Decides flagged cases and bans |
| Verification | Checks licence numbers against public registries and flags mismatches | Approves every verified badge |
| Member and business support | Answers common questions and drafts replies | Handles escalations |
| Partners and advertising | Drafts outreach and prepares partner reports | Negotiates and signs agreements |
| Church partnerships | Drafts event and workshop material | Builds the relationships and attends events |
| Bookkeeping | Categorises transactions and drafts monthly reports | Reviews them; an accountant files year-end returns |

### Pay

The founder's salary is $50K in 2027, $70K in 2028 and $90K in 2029, plus 8% for payroll taxes. There are no other staff; an accountant and a lawyer are paid as needed from the professional-fees line.

### Technology

- Next.js and React web app, responsive, meeting WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Managed cloud hosting, image storage and a CDN for vehicle photos.
- Licensed VIN-decoding and market-price data to power the price rating.
- AI tools and API usage for coding, moderation and support: $6K in 2027, rising to $24K in 2029 as moderation volume grows.

### Compliance

- **Dealer advertising:** Wayfold advertises vehicles and never sells them, so it does not need dealer registration. Dealer listings must follow OMVIC advertising rules, including all-in pricing.
- **Verification:** financing professionals and insurance brokers are checked against their licensing registries before they get a badge.
- **Privacy:** church affiliation is sensitive personal information under PIPEDA. It is opt-in, and members choose who can see it.
- **Paid links:** every ad, referral and affiliate link is labelled, so members always know when Wayfold is paid.
- **Referral fees:** a lawyer confirms that insurance and warranty referral payments are allowed under Ontario rules before any agreement is signed.
- **Accessibility and email:** AODA and WCAG 2.2 AA for the site; CASL consent for marketing email.

## Risks, funding and milestones

The biggest risk is slower member growth, because every revenue stream grows with members. $225K of starting capital covers even a 30% shortfall.

### Risks

| Risk | Effect if it happens | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Members grow more slowly than planned | At 70% of member targets, the low point deepens to $200K in April 2029 and 2029 profit falls to $32K | Starting capital covers this case; marketing shifts to the church channels that bring in the most members |
| Partners pay less than assumed | Each stream is a rate times members, so a lower rate cuts that stream in proportion | Sign at least two partners per stream; confirm payout rates before launch |
| Paid links erode trust | Members suspect ratings or answers are for sale | Every paid link and ad is labelled; partners never get placement, ratings or answers |
| One founder is a single point of failure | Illness or burnout stalls moderation and partner work | A stable product needs little upkeep, AI handles routine work, and written runbooks let a contractor step in, paid from the reserve |
| AI moderation misses scams or blocks good members | Trust or goodwill suffers | The founder decides every flagged case and ban; members can report and appeal |
| Referral rules limit insurance or lender payments | A stream shrinks or must change form | Legal review before signing; advertising and product commissions do not depend on those rules |
| The Christian focus reads as exclusive | Slower growth outside church networks | Anyone in the GTA can join; showing a church is optional, and Wayfold has no church content |

### Funding

- $225K of starting capital before the January 2027 launch, from founder savings, friends and family, or a small angel round.
- It covers the $130K low point in July 2028 with a $95K reserve, and still covers the $200K low point if members come in 30% below plan.
- The founder funds the MVP build from October to December 2026; that cost is outside the projection.
- No further raise is planned: Wayfold earns back its losses by October 2029.
