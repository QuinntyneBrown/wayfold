# User journeys

| Journey | Description | Complete | Estimate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Search vehicles | Anyone browses private and dealer listings filtered by make, price and municipality. | 100% | 5 days |
| View a vehicle | Anyone opens a vehicle's detail page; members message the seller. | 0% | 4 days |
| Manage a listing | A member or dealer creates, edits and removes a vehicle listing with photos. | 0% | 7 days |
| Check price rating | Anyone sees a Great, Good, Fair or High rating from comparable GTA listings. | 0% | 4 days |
| Register | A visitor creates an account and is signed in immediately. | 100% | 2 days |
| Sign in and sign out | A member signs in with email and password and signs out anytime. | 100% | 3 days |
| Find providers | Anyone finds providers by category, keyword and municipality, ranked by recommendations. | 0% | 4 days |
| Ask about a deal | A member asks a deal question; only verified financing professionals answer. | 0% | 5 days |
| Recommend | A member recommends a provider or product with a short written endorsement. | 0% | 5 days |
| Manage provider profile | A member creates and edits an auto provider profile in one of nine categories. | 0% | 6 days |
| Verify financing credential | A financing professional submits a credential and earns the Verified badge. | 0% | 5 days |
| Post and read the feed | A member publishes topic posts, reads the feed, likes and comments. | 100% | 6 days |
| Follow members | A member follows or unfollows another member from their profile. | 100% | 3 days |
| Manage garage | A member adds, edits and removes the cars they own. | 100% | 5 days |
| View member profile | A member views a profile's garage, posts and home church, and edits their bio. | 100% | 5 days |
| Discover products | Anyone browses recommended products by category and municipality. | 0% | 3 days |
| Manage account | A member changes display name, password, GTA area and optional home church. | 100% | 4 days |
| Navigate the app | Anyone moves through the responsive, keyboard-accessible shell and error pages. | 100% | 5 days |
