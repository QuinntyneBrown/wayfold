Pattern

Notifications

Wayfold tells members and dealers what just happened without moving them: a toast for the outcome of something they did (a recommendation posted, a vehicle listed), an alert in the page for anything they must read or act on. Two toast families exist in the mocks: community toasts on the feed and inventory toasts for dealers.

Overview

A notification confirms or warns about an action the person just took, then gets out of the way. It never carries information the person needs later: that belongs in the page (an alert, a field message, or the listing itself).

  • Community toasts on the home feed: new activity (someone followed you), a recommendation posted, a post that failed to send.
  • Inventory toasts on a dealer's inventory: draft saved, vehicle listed, photo upload delayed, listing not saved.
  • Toasts stack bottom-right from SM (bottom, full width on phones), newest last, at most three.

Structure

The region is in the DOM on page load, empty, so screen readers pick up toasts added later. Each toast has a title, an optional line of text, an optional action, a Dismiss button, and a progress bar only when it times out.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below SM (36rem): the region spans the bottom with --space-4 side insets and respects safe-area-inset-bottom.
  • From SM: a 24rem column bottom-right, --space-6 from the edges.
  • Long vehicle names wrap (overflow-wrap: anywhere); the toast grows taller, never wider.
  • Dismiss reaches 44 px on coarse pointers. Toasts are hidden in print.

Accessibility

  • Two regions exist on load: polite (role="status") for info, success and warning; assertive (role="alert") for danger. Never nest a role="alert" toast inside the polite region.
  • Toasts never take focus. Timers pause on hover and focus and are at least 6 s (WCAG 2.2.1); danger never times out.
  • Every Dismiss button names the toast it closes. Escape dismisses the newest toast when focus is inside the region.
  • Tone is carried by the title and icon, not colour alone (1.4.1). The inverse fill gets a 1 px edge in forced colours.
  • Toasts sit at --z-toast, below dialogs, and must not cover the focused control (2.4.11).

Content

  • Title says what happened, in past tense, with the item: "2021 Honda Civic Sport listed", not "Success".
  • Text says what it means or what to do next, in one sentence. Say that work is safe when it is: "Your post is still here."
  • No full stop on the title; full stop on the text. Sentence case. No "Oops" and no exclamation marks.
  • Failures start with "Could not" and name the thing; the action is "Try again".

Do and don't

Sources

  • Community toasts: info, success, warning, danger, stacked
  • Inventory toasts: info, success, warning, danger, stacked
  • In-page alerts: sign-in/locked, vehicles/error

Drift found in the mocks:

  • Danger toasts use role="alert" nested inside the role="status" region, so some screen readers announce them twice or politely. Resolved: a separate assertive region for danger.
  • No toast has a Dismiss button or icon, and the danger toasts have no timer and so cannot be closed. Resolved: every toast gets an icon and a named Dismiss; danger adds Try again (.toast__action, CSS need listed).
  • Danger copy "Could not save · Please try again." does not say what failed. Resolved: name the item and say the work is kept.
  • "Vehicle listed · Your inventory is up to date." does not name the vehicle. Resolved: "2021 Honda Civic Sport listed · It now appears in vehicle search."
