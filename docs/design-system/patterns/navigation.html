Pattern

Navigation and page structure

Every Wayfold page has the same frame: skip link, site header with the primary navigation, one main that opens with a page head, and the site footer. Below 768 pixels the primary links fold into a Menu sheet; signed-in members also get an account menu behind their avatar.

Overview

Navigation is flat. Three destinations are always one click away (Vehicles, Providers, Products), plus Feed when signed in. Account destinations (Your profile, Your garage, Settings, List your business, Sign out) live in the account menu. Inside a page, sibling views such as a member's Garage, For sale, Recommendations and Posts use a segmented link bar. There are no breadcrumbs in the mocks.

Structure

  1. Skip link "Skip to content", first focusable element, targets #main.
  2. header.site-header: wordmark (links home, or Feed when signed in), nav.site-nav labelled "Primary".
  3. main#main.container.stack with tabindex="-1", opening with .page-head (the only h1 and a one-line subtitle).
  4. footer.site-footer with its own nav labelled "Footer".

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 48rem (768 pixels) every .site-nav__extra item hides and .site-nav__menu shows; the avatar stays visible (L2-015 criterion 4).
  • Below 24rem the "Toronto" wordmark tag hides so the header never wraps.
  • The site menu is a full-height sheet on phones; the account menu is a popover at every width.
  • The segmented bar scrolls horizontally inside itself rather than wrapping the page.

Accessibility

  • Landmarks: one header, primary nav "Primary", main, footer nav "Footer". Each nav has a distinct label.
  • The sheet is role="dialog" with aria-modal="true" and labelled by its "Menu" heading; focus moves into it, is trapped, and Escape or Close returns focus to the Menu button.
  • The avatar button has a visible-to-screen-reader name "Account menu for Tendai Mensah"; the initials are aria-hidden.
  • Menu triggers are real buttons in production; the mocks use links only to move between static files.

Content

  • Nav labels are nouns, one or two words, sentence case: Vehicles, Providers, Products, Feed.
  • Header actions are verbs addressed to the member: Create account, Sell your car, List your business.
  • "Your" for the member's own things: Your profile, Your garage, Your inventory.
  • Page subtitles are one line that says what the page is for.

Do and don't

Sources

Mocks: site-menu/default, site-menu/signed-in, account-menu/default, member/default, every page header and footer. Requirement: L2-015. Components: Site header, Site footer, Skip link, Sheet, Popover, Segmented control, Section link.

Drift

  • site-menu and account-menu: "Your profile" and "Your garage" both link to member/own. Resolution: Your garage targets the garage view of the member's own profile.
  • Sign out is a link to home in both menus. Resolution: a button submitting a POST.
  • Menu and avatar triggers are anchors with aria-haspopup. Resolution: buttons in production; anchors are a static-mock convenience.
  • account-menu uses role="dialog" with aria-modal around a role="menu". Resolution: a non-modal popover containing the menu.
  • .breadcrumb--collapse exists in components.css but no mock uses breadcrumbs. Resolution: not part of the pattern.
