Pattern

Feedback and loading

How Wayfold tells members that something is happening, worked, or failed: skeleton cards while a page loads, busy buttons while a form sends, alerts inside the form or dialog that was used, and toasts for results that happen away from the member's focus.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable.skeleton.alert.toast

Overview

Feedback appears where the member is looking. Page loads keep the page head and replace content with a skeleton card plus a polite "Loading…" status. Submits change the primary button's label and disable it. Results inside a form or dialog are alerts at the top of that form; results of actions that finish elsewhere (a recommendation posted, a dealer listing saved) are toasts.

Responsive behaviour

  • Skeleton cards take the width of the content they replace; grids show the same number of placeholders per row as the loaded grid.
  • The toast region sits bottom-centre and full width minus the gutter below SM, bottom-right above it.
  • Alerts always span the form or dialog width; long text wraps, never truncates.

Accessibility

  • The toast region (role="status" aria-live="polite") is in the DOM before any toast; a danger toast sits outside it with its own role="alert", never nested in a polite region.
  • Skeletons are aria-hidden; only the status text is read. aria-busy on main is removed when content arrives.
  • Toasts that carry an action stay until dismissed; others stay at least 5 seconds and pause on hover and focus (WCAG 2.2.1).
  • Skeleton shimmer stops under prefers-reduced-motion.

Content

  • Loading and busy text ends in an ellipsis character: "Loading…", "Sending…", "Signing in…".
  • Success titles are past tense and short: "Saved", "Posted", "Recommendation posted", "Listing published".
  • Failures say what failed and what to do: "Could not save listing / Please try again." Never blame the member.

Drift found in the mocks:

  • inventory-toast/danger nests a role="alert" toast inside the polite status region. Danger toasts go in their own alert container.
  • ask-expert and contact-dealer success both say "Your message is available now". Copy names the audience per dialog.
  • Loading pages replace the heading with a generic one ("Vehicle detail") and settings changes its subtitle between loading and default. Keep the head identical.
