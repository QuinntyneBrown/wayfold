Shape

Pills for anything you press, soft corners for fields and cards, and vehicle photos at 4:3 so the whole car is in frame.

Overview

The radius grows with the element. Anything you press is a pill; fields and messages have 12px corners; cards, vehicle photos and dialogs have 20px. Vehicle photos are rectangles at 4:3 so the whole car is in frame.

True circles that stay circles at any size (badge dot, spinner) use a literal 50%.

Border widths

Two widths: a 1px hairline for every edge, 2px for marks that carry state or must read as a line. Focus is an outline, not a border; see Accessibility.

Usage

  • Pick the radius by element from the table, not by taste.
  • Pressable means pill. A square button reads as an input.
  • Nested corners shrink: a 12px alert inside a 20px dialog.
  • Square against an edge: the site menu sheet has no radius on the side touching the screen.

Accessibility

  • Radius never lowers the 3:1 edge requirement on controls (1.4.11).
  • Forced-colours mode keeps rounded controls visible with a 1px CanvasText edge.
  • A crop must not hide what the alt text names ("2021 Honda Civic Sport, front view").
