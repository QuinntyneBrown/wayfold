Iconography

Three glyphs, a 24-unit grid and a 2px stroke. Words do most of the work.

Overview

Wayfold uses very few icons. The mocks need three: a menu glyph that opens the site menu below 48rem, a close cross on dialogs and sheets, and a cross in the church tag. Words do the rest. Icons are inline SVG on a 24-unit grid, 2px stroke, round caps, drawn in currentColor.

An icon inside text matches the text: the church tag sizes its cross at 1em.

Usage

  • Add an icon only when it speeds recognition; never as decoration.
  • Pair with a visible label, except universally known actions (Close) which get an aria-label.
  • New icons: 24×24 viewBox, 2px stroke, round joins, no fills, no brand marks.

Accessibility

  • Every decorative icon has aria-hidden="true"; the control's name comes from text or aria-label (1.1.1, 4.1.2).
  • Meaningful icons hold 3:1 against their background (1.4.11).
  • Icon-only buttons keep the 44px target.
