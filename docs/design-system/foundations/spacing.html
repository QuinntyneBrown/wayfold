Spacing

A 4px scale. Small gaps tie a label to its control; large gaps separate home-page sections. White space does the dividing.

WCAG 2.2 AA15 steps4px base

Overview

A 4px base. Spacing grows with the thing it separates: 4px between a label and its control, 16px between cards in the vehicle grid, 20px inside a card, 64–96px between home-page sections. White space, not lines, separates sections.

16px grid gap, 16px card padding, 4px between meta lines.

Usage

  • Use .stack (16px) and .cluster (12px) instead of margins on children.
  • Inside-out: padding inside a component is never larger than the gap around it.
  • No off-scale values. If 20px and 24px both feel wrong, the layout is wrong.
