Content

Plain, warm and specific, like a neighbour who knows cars. Canadian formats, verbs on buttons, ratings with their basis, and church as a choice, never a rank.

Overview

Wayfold sounds like a trusted neighbour who knows cars: plain, warm and specific. It never pressures a sale and never preaches. Members are Christians in the GTA, and anyone in the GTA can join; faith shows up as the home church a member chooses to display, not as language on every screen.

Canadian formats

  • Canadian English: neighbour, favourite, centre, colour.
  • Prices: $24,995. Rates: 6.99%. Terms: 84 months.
  • Distance: 48,200 km.
  • Places: the municipality members know (Etobicoke, Scarborough, Brampton); the region is "Toronto and the GTA".
  • Dates: 15 October 2026.
  • Vehicles: year, make, model, trim: 2019 Toyota RAV4 XLE.

Buttons and links

Start with a verb and name the thing: "Sell your car", "Ask a question", "Follow Priya", "Add vehicle", "Delete vehicle". A destructive confirmation repeats the object ("Delete this vehicle?" → "Delete vehicle"), and Cancel is the safe default. Links say where they go: "View dealer profile", "See Priya's listing".

Price ratings and expert answers

  • Ratings are one word plus "price": Great price, Good price, Fair price, High price, always with the context: "Compared with 12 similar GTA listings." (L2-036).
  • No rating says why, in one sentence.
  • Expert answers carry the name and credential: "Marcus Chen · Verified financing professional". Answers inform; they never promise approval or a rate.
  • Questions are public, and the form says so before sending: "Your question and answers will be public."

Church and community

  • Home church is optional and shown as a tag with the church's own name ("Lakeshore Fellowship").
  • Never rank, filter out or label members by church or denomination; never imply members without one are less trustworthy.
  • Faith language is the member's own, in their posts; Wayfold's interface copy stays about cars.

Alt text

Name the vehicle and the view: "2021 Honda Civic Sport, front view". Do not repeat the price or say "image of".
