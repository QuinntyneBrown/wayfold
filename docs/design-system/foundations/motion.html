Foundation

Motion

Short, calm motion that shows where a dialog, sheet or toast came from, and none at all when a member asks for less.

Overview

Motion explains where something came from: a dialog rises from the bottom on a phone and scales in on larger screens, a toast slides up, a skeleton shimmers while a vehicle list loads. It is short, calm and never decorative. With reduced motion every transition collapses to almost zero.

Reduced motion

Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the four transition durations become 0.01ms, so components need no extra queries. The skeleton shimmer stops; the busy spinner keeps turning because it reports status (2.3.3, 2.2.2).

Usage

  • Enter with --ease-enter, leave with --ease-exit, change in place with --ease-standard.
  • Nothing moves on its own for more than five seconds except status indicators.
  • Animate opacity and transform, not layout.

Accessibility

  • Respects prefers-reduced-motion (2.3.3).
  • No flashing; loops are slow and low-contrast (2.3.1).
  • Toasts that auto-dismiss pause on hover and focus, and errors persist until dismissed (2.2.1).
