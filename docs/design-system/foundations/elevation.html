Elevation

Flat by default. Borders separate cards; shadows only lift what floats over the page.

WCAG 2.2 AA4 shadows9 layers

Overview

Wayfold is flat. Cards and vehicle cards sit on the page with a 1px border and no shadow; white space groups them. Shadows only lift things that float over the page: a hovered primary button, the account menu, toasts, dialogs and the site menu sheet.

In dark mode the shadows are darker and stronger, and raised surfaces also lighten to --color-bg-surface-raised (stone-850), because a shadow alone is invisible on a near-black page.

Usage

  • Elevation means "floats over the page". Never use it to make a card look important.
  • Pair z-order with the layer's shadow: a modal is --z-modal and --shadow-4.
  • Use the z tokens; never a literal z-index.

Accessibility

  • Shadows are decorative; every floating surface also has an edge or a contrasting fill, and forced-colours mode adds a 1px CanvasText outline to dialogs, sheets and toasts.
  • Stacking follows focus: a dialog traps focus, so nothing below it can be reached (2.4.3).
