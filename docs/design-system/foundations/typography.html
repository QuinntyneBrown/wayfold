Typography

One sans family, nine sizes and sixteen roles. The price is the loudest thing on a card; the facts that decide a sale are small but never faint.

Overview

One sans-serif family in four weights, and a mono face for code. Type is set in rem so it follows the member's browser size. Prices are the largest thing on a card (--text-h3), the vehicle title sits under them, and the facts that decide a sale (kilometres, municipality, rating) are small but never faint: they use --color-fg-muted, not subtle.

Components set type with the font shorthand of a role token (font: var(--text-label)), never with loose sizes and weights.

Weight, leading and tracking

Regular for reading, medium for labels and titles that sit in body text, semibold for small headings and the current link, bold for the big headings. Headings are tight (1.1–1.3) and balanced with text-wrap: balance; body is 1.5 and paragraphs stop at --layout-measure (62ch).

Prices, distances and dates

  • Prices in Canadian dollars with a thousands comma and no cents: $24,995.
  • Distances in kilometres with a comma and a space: 48,200 km.
  • Vehicle titles are year, make, model, trim: 2021 Honda Civic Sport.
  • Facts in a row are joined with a middle dot and spaces: 71,000 km · Etobicoke · Fair price.

More rules on the Content page.

Usage

  • One h1 per page, then h2 for sections and cards. Do not skip levels for size; pick the role.
  • Never below 12px. --font-size-xs is the floor, and only for captions, badges and tags.
  • Never set type in px. Roles are rem so 200% zoom works (1.4.4).

Accessibility

  • All sizes are rem; layouts reflow at 320 CSS px and 400% zoom (1.4.4, 1.4.10, L2-015.1).
  • Line height 1.5 for body and paragraph widths capped at 62ch support text-spacing overrides (1.4.12).
  • Headings are real h1–h4 in order; the page title matches the h1.
