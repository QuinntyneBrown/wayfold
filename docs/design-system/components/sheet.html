Sheet

A full-height panel for the site menu: full screen on a phone, a 28rem panel from the right edge on wider screens. It holds the destinations that do not fit the phone header.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable.sheetrole="dialog" aria-modal

Overview

Below 48rem the header collapses to the wordmark and Menu (L2-015.4). Menu opens the site menu: a sheet listing every header destination as section links, plus the account links that the avatar holds on wider screens. Use a dialog for a task and a popover for a short menu anchored to its trigger.

Anatomy

  1. Backdrop.backdrop: dims the page from 48rem, where the sheet does not cover it.
  2. Header.sheet__header: title "Menu" (h2) and a ghost Close at the end, hairline below.
  3. Body.sheet__body: scrolls; holds the section list and, signed in, Sign out.

Sizes

Full screen below 48rem; min(100%, var(--layout-sheet-width)) (28rem) from 48rem.

States

A sheet is open or closed; its rows and Close carry their own states (see Section link and Button). Open, the Menu button has aria-expanded="true".

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 48rem: full screen, slides up; the header pads for the top safe area.
  • From 48rem: docked right, 28rem, slides in from the right with --shadow-4 and a backdrop. In practice the site menu is only reachable below 48rem, because Menu hides there.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A modal dialog (role="dialog" aria-modal="true" aria-labelledby) holding <nav aria-label="Main">. The page behind is inert.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabClose, then each row, then Sign out; focus stays in the sheet.
EscCloses; focus returns to Menu.

Focus

On open, focus goes to Close. Rows show an inset ring so the scroll area does not clip it.

Content

  • Title "Menu"; Close says "Close" (icon plus word).
  • Rows use the header's names, in the header's order, then account links.

Drift found:

  • No .backdrop behind the sheet. Resolution: add it.
  • Close is <a href="home/default" aria-label="Close menu">Close</a>. Resolution: a button named by its visible word.
  • The header h2 had no style, so Close sat beside the title. Resolution: .sheet__header > h2 takes the free space (in css-comp-nav.css).
  • Sign out is a link row. Resolution: a ghost button in a form below the list.
