Popover

A small raised panel anchored to its trigger. Wayfold uses one: the account menu under the header avatar.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable.popover.account-menuDisclosure

Overview

The account menu opens from the avatar from 48rem (below that, its links are in the site menu). It names the member and lists Your profile, Settings, List your business and Sign out. It is not modal: the page stays usable and a click outside closes it. Use a dialog for anything with fields.

Anatomy

  1. Trigger.avatar-btn with aria-expanded="true" while open.
  2. Panel.popover: raised surface, hairline border, --radius-md, --shadow-3, 8px below the header at the inline end.
  3. NameThe member's name as a small muted heading.
  4. Links44px rows; Sign out last, after a hairline, as a button.

Variants

One variant. Wayfold has no tooltips or other anchored panels; add a variant only when a mock needs one.

Sizes

At least 14rem, at most min(20rem, 90vw); rows are 44px tall.

States

First row hovered (forced): --color-bg-subtle.

Closed, the panel is not rendered and the avatar has aria-expanded="false".

Responsive behaviour

  • Shown from 48rem only, because the avatar hides below that; the same links are in the site menu.
  • Anchored to the header's inline end at the page margin, so it never runs off-screen; width caps at 90vw.

Theming

Raised surface, hairline border and shadow; all semantic tokens.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A disclosure (APG Disclosure): the avatar is a <button aria-expanded aria-controls>; the panel holds a labelled <nav> of links. It is not an ARIA menu (that would demand arrow-key handling and hide the links' link role) and not a modal dialog.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter / Space on the avatarOpens; focus stays on the avatar and the next Tab enters the panel.
TabThrough the links and Sign out; tabbing past the last closes it.
EscCloses; focus returns to the avatar.

Contrast

Link on popover
Name on popover
Focus ring on popover

Hover is a fill change; in forced colours it becomes an outline.

Content

  • The heading is the member's display name.
  • Rows: "Your profile", "Settings", "List your business", "Sign out": the same words as the site menu.

Code

<button class="avatar-btn" type="button" aria-expanded="true" aria-controls="account-menu">
  <span class="avatar" aria-hidden="true">TM</span><span class="visually-hidden">Account menu for Tendai Mensah</span>
</button>
<div class="popover account-menu" id="account-menu">
  <h2>Tendai Mensah</h2>
  <nav aria-label="Account"><ul>
    <li><a href="/members/me">Your profile</a></li>
    <li><a href="/settings">Settings</a></li>
    <li><a href="/providers/new">List your business</a></li>
    <li><form method="post" action="/sign-out"><button class="btn btn--ghost btn--block" type="submit">Sign out</button></form></li>
  </ul></nav>
</div>
Class / attributeEffect
.popoverAnchored raised panel.
.account-menuPlaces it under the avatar and styles the name, rows and divider (css-comp-nav.css).

Drift found:

  • The panel is role="dialog" aria-modal="true" wrapping role="menu". Resolution: a non-modal disclosure with a nav of links.
  • .account-menu has no CSS, so the panel sat at the top-left of the page. Resolution: placement and row styles added in css-comp-nav.css.
  • "Your profile" and "Your garage" both go to member/own. Resolution: one row.
  • Sign out is a link to home/default. Resolution: a button that posts.
