Toast

A toast is a short message in the corner after something finishes in the background: a recommendation posted, a vehicle listed, a save that failed.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable.toast-region.toast.toast--info.toast--success.toast--warning.toast--danger

Overview

Use a toast for community and inventory events (notifications/community-toast, inventory-toast). Info, success and warning dismiss themselves (shown by .toast__progress); danger stays until dismissed.

Anatomy

  1. Region.toast-region, fixed bottom right (bottom on phones).
  2. Title.toast__title.
  3. Text.toast__text.
  4. Progress.toast__progress: time left.

Sizes

One size: 24rem wide from 36rem; full width minus --space-4 on phones.

States

  • Entering: slide-up over --duration-base.
  • Shown: progress bar counts down.
  • Leaving: data-state="leaving" fades out.
  • Stacked: newest last, gap --space-3.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 36rem the region spans the bottom with --space-4 margins and respects the safe area.
  • From 36rem it is 24rem wide at the bottom right.

Theming

All tones share the inverse surface (--color-bg-inverse/--color-fg-inverse); the tone is in the words. In forced colours a 1px edge is added.

Accessibility

Info, success and warning go in a role="status" aria-live="polite" region. Danger goes in a separate role="alert" region and is not auto-dismissed. Toasts never take focus. Pause the timer on hover and focus so users have enough time (WCAG 2.2.1).

Toast text

Content

  • Title: past tense of what happened: "Vehicle listed".
  • Text: one short sentence, with next step if any.
  • No actions in auto-dismissing toasts.
