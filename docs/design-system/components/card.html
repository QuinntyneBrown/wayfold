Component · Data display

Card

A card is the bordered surface that groups one topic: a vehicle's details, the price rating, a seller, a product, a post. It is the most-used container in Wayfold.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable.card.card.stack

Overview

Use a card to group content about one thing so it reads as a unit: "Vehicle details", the price panel, the seller panel, "Ask about this deal", each product in the directory, each post and recommendation. Do not use a card as page chrome or to frame a whole form that already sits alone on the page.

  • Section card (section.card.stack) has its own h2 and stacks its children with --space-* gaps.
  • List card (li.card.stack) is one item in .directory-grid: products and providers.
  • Composed cards add a role class: .post, .reco, .member-head, .garage-card. They keep the same surface.
  • Vehicles use their own vehicle card, which bleeds the photo to the edge.

Anatomy

  1. Surface--color-bg-surface with a hairline --color-border-default edge and --radius-lg.
  2. HeadingAn h2 (or h3 inside a section) that names the card; margin removed by .card h2.
  3. ContentChildren spaced by .stack; padding --space-5.

Sizes

One size. Width comes from the layout (.detail-layout, .directory-grid, .garage-grid); padding is always --space-5.

States

A card is not interactive, so it has no hover or focus state of its own. Its content has states:

  • Default: content as above.
  • Loading: the card holds skeleton lines and is aria-hidden.
  • Sold / unavailable: the card says so in words ("Sold. This vehicle is no longer available.").
  • No rating: the price card keeps the price and explains why there is no rating.

Responsive behaviour

  • Cards never set a width. In .detail-layout they sit in a 2:1 grid from 48rem and stack below it.
  • .directory-grid and .garage-grid fill columns of at least 18rem, so a phone shows one card per row and nothing scrolls sideways at 320px.
  • Long names and prices wrap inside the card; padding stays --space-5.

Theming

The card reads --color-bg-surface and --color-border-default. In dark the surface lifts one step above the canvas so the edge is not the only separation.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Use <section> with a heading when the card is a page region, <li> when it is one of many, <article> for a post or recommendation. No ARIA role is added. The card is never one big link; its links and buttons are separate targets (L2-016).

Headings

Card headings continue the page outline: h2 under the page h1, h3 for an item inside a section card ("Is 6.99% over 84 months a good offer?").

Contrast

Body text on card
Muted text on card

The hairline border is decorative (WCAG 1.4.11 does not apply); the heading and spacing identify the card.

Content

  • Heading names the thing, not the action: "Vehicle details", "Ask about this deal", the dealer's name.
  • One topic per card. Split "price" and "seller" into two cards, as the vehicle page does.
  • Put the one action last, as a link or button ("View dealer profile", "Message seller").
