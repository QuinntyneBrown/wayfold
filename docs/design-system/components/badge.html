Badge

A badge is a short status word with a dot. Wayfold uses it for a vehicle's automatic price rating.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable.badge.badge--success

Overview

Use a badge for the automatic price rating on a vehicle page (L2-036): "Good price" in success, "Fair price" neutral. It is read-only. When there are not enough comparable listings, there is no badge; a sentence says why.

Anatomy

  1. Dot::before, current colour; the shape that does not rely on colour alone.
  2. LabelThe rating in words.

Variants

Good price Fair price
Success and neutral, from pages/vehicle-detail/default and private.
HTML
<span class="badge badge--success">Good price</span> <span class="badge">Fair price</span>

Sizes

One size: --font-size-xs label, min height --target-min.

States

Rated and no-rating price cards.

<section class="card stack"><h2>$24,995</h2><span class="badge badge--success">Good price</span><p>Compared with 12 similar GTA listings.</p></section><section class="card stack"><h2>$24,995</h2><p>Not enough comparable GTA listings for a price rating yet.</p></section>

Responsive behaviour

  • Never wraps; it sits on its own line in the price card (.stack > .badge aligns it to the start).

Theming

Neutral uses --color-bg-subtle/--color-fg-muted; success uses --color-success-bg/--color-success-fg.

Both themes

Good priceFair price

Accessibility

Inline text, not focusable, no role. The word carries the meaning; colour adds to it. The sentence under it ("Compared with 12 similar GTA listings.") explains the basis.

Contrast

Success badge
Neutral badge

Content

  • Two words, sentence case, ending in "price".
  • Always follow it with the comparison count.
