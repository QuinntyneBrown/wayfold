Tag and church tag

A tag is a small outlined label. The church tag is the tag Wayfold uses to show a member's home church as a trust signal.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable.tag.church-tag

Overview

The church tag (.tag.church-tag) appears beside a member's name on their profile, on feed posts, on recommendations and on a private seller's panel when the member chose to show their church (L2-022). It is not a link and not a filter. Plain .tag is the base it builds on.

Anatomy

  1. OutlineHairline --color-border-default, --radius-full.
  2. IconCross glyph at 1em, aria-hidden.
  3. LabelChurch name in --text-body-sm, muted.

Sizes

One size: text --text-body-sm; minimum height --target-min so it lines up with small buttons in a row.

States

  • Shown: the member chose to show their church.
  • Hidden: no tag at all, not "No church".

Responsive behaviour

  • Does not wrap internally (white-space: nowrap); a long church name moves the whole tag to the next line.
  • Very long names should still fit at 320px; the label is the church's short name.

Theming

Muted text on a transparent fill with a hairline edge, in both themes.

Accessibility

Plain inline text: the screen reader reads the church name after the member's name. The icon is aria-hidden. Because the outline alone does not say "church", context comes from its position beside the name; on a member page the bio sentence makes it explicit.

Content

  • The church's own short name: "Lakeshore Fellowship".
  • No denomination labels or ratings.
