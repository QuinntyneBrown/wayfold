Site footer

The quiet end of every browse and profile page: the Wayfold name and line, a short row of links, and the copyright.

Overview

The footer repeats the main sections so a member who has scrolled a long vehicle list or feed can move on without going back up. It holds no actions, forms or church content. It appears on browse, detail, profile, settings and error pages; sign-in and sign-up leave it out like they leave out the header links.

Anatomy

  1. Brand.site-footer__brand: "Wayfold" in --text-h4 and the line "Cars, care and community across Toronto and the GTA."
  2. Links.site-footer__links: a labelled <nav> list, stacked on phones and in a row from 36rem.
  3. Copyright.site-footer__copy: a full-width last line.

Variants

One variant. Signed-in and signed-out pages show the same footer; Settings sends a visitor to sign-in.

Sizes

One size: body-small text, 44px tall link targets, 40px padding above and below.

Hover (forced with data-state): default ink and underline.

Focus uses the shared ring. There are no other states.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 36rem the links stack in one column of 44px rows; from 36rem they run in a row with 24px between.
  • Brand and links sit side by side when there is room and wrap otherwise; the copyright always takes the last full line.
  • Bottom padding adds env(safe-area-inset-bottom) so the last line clears a phone's home indicator.

Theming

The footer has no fill; it uses muted text on the canvas and moves to default ink on hover in both themes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

One <footer> per page, outside <main>, so it is the contentinfo landmark. Its links sit in <nav aria-label="Footer">, distinct from the header's "Primary".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabEach footer link in order, after the page content.

Contrast

Footer text on page
Brand name on page

Hover adds an underline, so the change does not rely on colour.

Content

  • The brand line is fixed: "Cars, care and community across Toronto and the GTA."
  • Links are the browse sections plus Settings; no legal pages until they exist.
  • Copyright: "© 2026 Wayfold · Toronto and the GTA" with a middle dot, not a hyphen.

Drift found:

  • All 77 footers use "© 2026 Wayfold - Toronto and the GTA" with a hyphen. Resolution: middle dot.
  • No footer link is ever marked current even on the Vehicles, Providers, Products and Settings pages. Resolution: mark it with aria-current="page".
