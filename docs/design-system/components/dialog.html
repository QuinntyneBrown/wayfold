Dialog

A modal panel for a short task that keeps the page underneath: message a seller, ask about a deal, recommend a provider, add a car to your garage, or confirm deleting a listing.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable.dialog.dialog__panel.backdroprole="dialog" aria-modal

Overview

Open a dialog when the task is short, belongs to the page behind it, and the member should come back to that page after: "Message the seller" from a vehicle, "Ask about this deal" (L2-037), "Recommend Danforth Auto Care" (L2-026), "Add a car to your garage" (L2-024), "Delete this vehicle?" (L2-007). Anything longer, or with its own URL, is a page (the vehicle editor). Navigation on phones uses a sheet; the account menu is a popover.

Anatomy

  1. Backdrop.backdrop: --color-bg-backdrop over the page, which is inert.
  2. Panel.dialog__panel: raised surface, --radius-lg, --shadow-4; a bottom sheet with top corners on phones.
  3. Header.dialog__header with .dialog__heading: .dialog__title (h2) and .dialog__desc; Close at the end.
  4. Body.dialog__body: fields, an alert, or nothing.
  5. Footer.dialog__footer: Cancel, then the confirming action last; sticky at the bottom when the body scrolls.

Default, invalid, busy, failed and success are the five states every Wayfold dialog has. The panel itself has no hover state.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 36rem the panel docks to the bottom, full width, with rounded top corners, and slides up.
  • From 36rem it is centred with 24px around it and scales in.
  • Footer buttons wrap; the confirming action stays last. The panel adds the safe-area inset at the bottom.

Theming

The panel is --color-bg-surface-raised, which lifts above the canvas in both themes; the backdrop dims the page.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="dialog" aria-modal="true" with aria-labelledby on the title and aria-describedby on the description (APG Modal dialog). The page behind is inert. Form dialogs wrap body and footer in a <form> so Enter submits.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabCycles through the dialog's controls only.
EscCloses, like Cancel, unless busy.
EnterSubmits a form dialog.

Focus

On open, focus goes to the first field (form dialogs) or Cancel (confirmations, so Enter never deletes). On close, focus returns to the button that opened it.

Contrast

Title on panel
Description on panel
Focus ring on panel

In forced colours the shadow is gone, so the panel gets a 1px outline.

Motion

Slide-up or scale-in over --duration-slow; reduced motion removes it.

Content

  • Title says the task: "Message the seller", "Ask about this deal", "Recommend Danforth Auto Care". A confirmation asks a question: "Delete this vehicle?"
  • Description: one sentence on what happens or who sees it: "Your question and answers will be public."
  • Buttons: "Cancel" then a verb phrase naming the result: "Send message", "Post question", "Delete vehicle". Success closes with "Done".
  • Success copy says what happened to this item: "Message sent", not a generic "Posted".

Drift found:

  • Only delete-vehicle has a Close button; the 20 form-dialog mocks have none. Resolution: Close in every header.
  • delete-vehicle/busy changes the button to "Deleting…". Resolution: keep "Delete vehicle" with aria-busy; the status line carries the progress.
  • contact-dealer/success says "Posted · Your message is available now." and "Done" links to the feed. Resolution: "Message sent", and Done closes the dialog.
  • Form dialogs are not <form>s and their submit is type="button". Resolution: the panel is the form; the action is type="submit".
  • "2000 characters" without a separator. Resolution: "2,000" (Canadian English).
