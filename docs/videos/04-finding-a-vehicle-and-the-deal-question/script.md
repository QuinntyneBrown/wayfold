# 04 · Finding a vehicle and the deal question

This video shows how you find a car on Wayfold and how you find out whether the price, or the financing, is a good deal. The screens are mocks of the product, as of October 2026.

## Search every seller

Vehicles is one search across private sellers and every dealer in the GTA. You can filter by seller type, by make and by GTA municipality. In the mock there are two hundred and forty-eight vehicles, newest first.

Every card shows the price, the mileage, where the car is, who is selling it, and a price rating. Priya's RAV4 is a fair price. Eglinton Auto Group's Civic is a good price, and their Mazda3 a great price.

If your filters are too narrow, Wayfold says no matches and gives you one button to clear them.

## The vehicle page

Every vehicle has its own page. Here is the 2021 Honda Civic Sport from Eglinton Auto Group, at twenty-four thousand nine hundred and ninety-five dollars.

The price rating sits next to the price: good price, compared with twelve similar GTA listings. Below are the details: mileage, condition, location and features.

When there aren't enough similar cars in the GTA, Wayfold says so instead of guessing. No rating is better than a wrong one.

## Ask about this deal

Here is the part no other car site has. Under the details, anyone can ask a public question about the deal.

Someone asked: is six point nine nine percent over eighty-four months a good offer? Marcus Chen, a verified financing professional, answered: compare the total cost with your bank or credit union before deciding.

To ask your own, choose a topic, like is the price fair, and write up to a thousand characters. Questions and answers are public, so the next buyer learns from yours.

An empty question gets a gentle nudge to write one. A good one gets posted right away.

On a car nobody has asked about yet, the section invites you to be the first.

## Message the seller

When you're ready, message the seller. It's a private note to the dealer or the private seller, up to two thousand characters, and you see a clear confirmation when it's sent.

## Things to remember

- One search covers private sellers and every GTA dealer.
- Every car carries a price rating, and Wayfold says when it doesn't have enough data.
- Anyone can ask a public question about a deal, and verified financing professionals answer.
- Message the seller when you're ready.
