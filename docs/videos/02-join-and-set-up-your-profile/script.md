# 02 · Join and set up your profile

This video walks you through joining Wayfold: creating an account, signing in, choosing what other members see, and what happens when something goes wrong. Everything you see is a mock of the product, as of October 2026, so the screens are final in design but not yet live.

## The front door

You start on the home page. It says what Wayfold is: find your people in the GTA auto community. You can browse cars for sale, providers and products without an account. To post, follow people or keep a garage, you create one.

## Create your account

The sign-up form asks for three things: a display name, an email and a password. Anyone in the GTA can join.

If you miss something, the form tells you exactly what to fix. A box at the top says check these fields, and each field says what it needs: enter your name, enter a valid email, use at least twelve characters for the password.

While the account is being created, the button shows that it's working, so you don't press it twice.

Then you're in. The next step is right there on the screen: add your first car to your garage.

## Sign in

Coming back is the same idea: email and password. Mistakes get the same field-by-field help.

After too many wrong tries, Wayfold pauses sign-in for fifteen minutes. It's a simple guard against someone guessing passwords.

## Settings and your home church

In settings, you choose what members see. Here's Tendai Mensah. He has a display name, a GTA area, and an optional home church, Lakeshore Fellowship.

Showing your church is your choice. A checkbox decides if signed-in members can see it, and signed-out visitors never see it at all. It's a trust signal, not a gate.

The church name has a limit of eighty characters, and the form says so if you go over.

When you save, a green message confirms that your profile and church visibility are updated.

## What others see

This is how a profile looks to other members. Priya Nair from Etobicoke shows her church, Lakeshore Fellowship, under her name. Her garage, her cars for sale, her recommendations and her posts each have their own tab, and there's a button to follow her.

## On your phone

Wayfold works the same on a phone. The menu folds into one button and opens a full list: your feed, sell your car, your garage, and the rest.

And if a page doesn't exist, or the site has a problem, you get a plain message and one button back home.

## Things to remember

- Anyone in the GTA can join with a name, an email and a password.
- Every form says exactly what to fix, field by field.
- Too many wrong passwords pause sign-in for fifteen minutes.
- Your home church is optional, and you choose who sees it.
