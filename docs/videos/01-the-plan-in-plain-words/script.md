# 01 · The plan, in plain words

Picture one place where people in Toronto and the GTA who buy, sell and look after cars can find each other. You can see who is selling, what they drive, and which mechanic they trust. That place is Wayfold. And here is the surprising part: nobody on Wayfold pays. Not members, not dealers, not mechanics.

So how does a free place pay the bills? This video explains the business plan in plain words, as of October 2026, with the numbers for the first three years.

## The problem

Buying a car is a trust problem. Today's car sites are big, but big isn't the same as trusted.

Here is one number. From 2016 to 2020, Ontario's car-dealer regulator handled about five thousand four hundred complaints against dealers. About half ended with nothing fixed for the buyer.

And the market is huge. About seven point one million people live in the GTA, and there are roughly three thousand eight hundred dealers. Wayfold only needs forty-five thousand members, less than one in a hundred people.

## What you get, free

Here is what everyone gets, for free.

Members get a profile, a garage of the cars they own, a feed of car posts, and free private-sale listings. A member can also show their home church. It's a trust signal, not a gate. Anyone in the GTA can join.

Every vehicle, from a private seller or a dealer, gets its own page with a price rating. It tells you if the price is good, fair or high, compared with similar cars in the GTA.

Mechanics, body shops and other providers get a profile that collects recommendations from members you can actually see.

And verified financing professionals answer public questions, like this one: is six point nine nine percent over eighty-four months a good offer?

## Who pays

So who pays? Outside companies that want to reach GTA car buyers. Think of four money pipes.

The first pipe is car loans. When a member applies through Wayfold and a partner lender funds the loan, the lender pays about three hundred dollars.

The second pipe is insurance and warranties. An insurer pays about twenty-five dollars for a finished quote, and a warranty company pays about one hundred and fifty for a warranty sold.

The third pipe is ads. Clearly labelled ads on search and vehicle pages, about eight dollars for every thousand page views.

The fourth pipe is products. When a member buys a recommended product through Wayfold's link, the store pays a small commission. About two dollars and fifty cents per member a year.

One rule protects all four. Every paid link says it's paid, and nobody can buy a better price rating or a higher spot in search.

## Three years

Now the three years. Year one is 2027.

In 2027, revenue is about forty-two thousand dollars. The pipes only open in April, and members are still arriving.

In 2028, revenue grows to about one hundred and ninety-three thousand.

In 2029, it reaches about four hundred and seventy-six thousand dollars. Car-loan referrals are the biggest pipe, at thirty-eight percent.

Costs grow too, but slower. They go from one hundred and thirty-eight thousand to about three hundred thousand. So 2027 loses about ninety-six thousand dollars, 2028 loses about seventeen thousand, and 2029 earns a profit of about one hundred and seventy-four thousand.

Why does it grow? Every pipe grows with one number: members. Six thousand at the end of 2027, twenty thousand at the end of 2028, and forty-five thousand at the end of 2029.

## The cash

Losing money first means you need money in the bank. The plan starts with two hundred and twenty-five thousand dollars.

The lowest point comes in July 2028, at about one hundred and thirty thousand dollars spent. That still leaves ninety-five thousand in reserve.

August 2028 is the first month that makes a profit, and every month after it does too.

By October 2029, all the early losses are paid back. By December 2029, Wayfold brings in about fifty-three thousand dollars a month and keeps about twenty-eight thousand of it.

## Getting members

Members come through churches and search, not a sales team.

The founder partners with fifteen GTA churches in 2027, forty by the end of 2028 and seventy by the end of 2029. Each gets a short talk, a car-care workshop or a free inspection day with a member mechanic.

Dealers join because listing is free and the members are already there. Recommendations bring in mechanics and shops. And from 2028, search becomes the main way new members find Wayfold, because every vehicle page and every public answer is a page search engines can find.

Here is the whole timeline. Build the site this fall. Launch in January 2027. Turn on partners and ads in April. First profitable month in August 2028. Everything paid back by October 2029.

## One founder

Wayfold is run by one person working with AI.

The AI does the routine work: fixes, screening listings for scams, checking licence numbers, drafting replies and bookkeeping. The founder makes every decision about trust, money and the law.

That only works because the product stays the same. Like Kijiji or a Reddit forum, Wayfold launches with every feature and then holds still. After launch, the only changes are fixes, security and legal needs. No feature roadmap means no engineering team to hire.

## The risk

The biggest risk is simple: members come more slowly than planned.

Say only seventy percent of the members show up. The lowest point gets deeper, about two hundred thousand dollars in April 2029, and the 2029 profit drops to about thirty-two thousand. The starting money still covers it.

The other risks have answers too. Sign at least two partners for each pipe. Label every paid link. And keep a written runbook so someone can step in if the founder is sick.

## Things to remember

- Nobody on Wayfold pays. Outside companies pay to reach GTA car buyers.
- Four pipes: car loans, insurance and warranties, labelled ads, and product links.
- Revenue grows from about forty-two thousand to about four hundred and seventy-six thousand dollars in three years.
- The first profitable month is August 2028, and two hundred and twenty-five thousand dollars of starting money covers the dip.
- Members are the number to watch.
