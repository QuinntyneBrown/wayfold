# 03 · Your garage and selling your car

On Wayfold, your cars live in your garage. This video shows how you add a car, share it with the community, and turn it into a private sale with a few clicks. The screens are mocks of the product, as of October 2026.

## Your garage

This is Tendai Mensah's own profile. Because it's his, he sees two buttons the public doesn't: edit profile and add a car. His garage holds one car, a 2015 Honda Fit he calls Bluebird.

## Add a car

Add a car opens a short form: year, make and model, plus an optional nickname, photo and notes.

Year, make and model are required. Leave them out and the form marks each one and tells you what's missing.

If the network drops while you save, Wayfold says it could not send, and your text is still there. Nothing you typed is lost.

## Share it

Your car can show up in the community feed. Members post about their cars, ask questions and share recommendations, and a small message at the corner confirms when a post goes live.

## Sell this car

When it's time to sell, you don't start from scratch. Sell your car opens a listing that starts from Bluebird in your garage: the year, make and model are already filled in. You add the price, the mileage, the condition and where in the GTA the car is.

If the year or price doesn't make sense, the form says check the listing and marks the fields to fix.

Publish, and your car is listed. It's now in vehicle search, next to every dealer's inventory, and members can message you.

## What buyers see

Here is a private listing from the buyer's side: Priya Nair's 2019 Toyota RAV4 XLE at twenty-nine thousand five hundred dollars.

Wayfold rates the price against eight similar GTA listings and calls it a fair price. Next to it is the seller: Priya, a private seller in Etobicoke, with her church shown to signed-in members, and a button to message her.

When a car sells, the page stays up and says sold, so links people shared don't break.

## Things to remember

- Your garage holds the cars you own; only you see edit profile and add a car.
- Year, make and model are all a car needs.
- A failed save keeps your text.
- Sell this car starts the listing from your garage, so you only add price, mileage, condition and place.
- Every listing gets a price rating and a way to message the seller.
