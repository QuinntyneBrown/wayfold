# 05 · Providers and recommendations

Finding an honest mechanic usually depends on who you know. On Wayfold, it depends on who you can see. This video shows the provider directory, how you recommend a business, and how product recommendations work. The screens are mocks of the product, as of October 2026.

## The provider directory

Providers lists the auto people and businesses of the GTA: dealers, mechanics, body shops, tire shops, detailers, towing, insurance, financing and parts. You can filter by category and by municipality.

The list is ranked by recommendations from members. Danforth Auto Care, a mechanic in Toronto, leads with twenty-eight. Then Eglinton Auto Group with nineteen, Marcus Chen with fifteen, and Steeles Brake and Tire with eleven.

Too narrow a search gives you no matches, and one button to clear the filters.

## A provider page

Here is Danforth Auto Care: honest repairs and clear explanations for Toronto drivers. The owner shows the church he attends, St. Paul's Anglican, to signed-in members. And under member recommendations, you can read what people said, with their names, like Priya Nair: they explained the repair clearly, gave me options and finished on time.

## Recommend a business

To recommend Danforth yourself, press recommend and write between ten and five hundred characters. It's a few honest sentences, not a star rating.

Too short, and the form asks for at least ten characters. Post it, and it's live.

Your recommendation then shows up in three places: on the provider's page, on the recommendations tab of your own profile, and in the community feed for people who follow you.

## Products

Products work the same way, with one difference: products are not sold on Wayfold. Members recommend what they use and say where they bought it. Michelin X-Ice Snow winter tires, recommended by thirty-two members and bought at Steeles Brake and Tire. WeatherTech floor liners, a Viofo dash cam and a Chemical Guys detailing kit.

To recommend a product, you give its name, a category, where you bought it and why you recommend it.

## Things to remember

- Providers are ranked by recommendations from members you can see.
- A recommendation is ten to five hundred characters of honest words, not stars.
- It appears on the provider page, your profile and the feed.
- Products are recommended, never sold, on Wayfold.
