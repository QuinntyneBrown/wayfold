# 06 · Dealers and financing professionals

Dealers and financing professionals use Wayfold for free, like everyone else. This video shows how a business gets a profile, how a dealer manages inventory, and how a financing professional becomes verified. The screens are mocks of the product, as of October 2026.

## List your business

Any auto business starts with list your business. One profile works for every kind of provider: you give a name, pick a category, choose your GTA municipality, say what you do and add a contact email.

Financing professionals add one more thing: their licence or credential.

Leave out the name or the description and the form asks you to complete the required fields.

Once you submit, the profile is saved. The next steps say that financing professionals appear as verified only after Wayfold checks their licence.

## A dealer's profile

Here is Eglinton Auto Group, a dealer in Scarborough, recommended by nineteen members. Its page carries the same recommend and call buttons as any provider, and below them, its inventory.

## Managing inventory

The dealer manages its cars from your inventory. A new dealer starts with an empty list.

Add vehicle opens the same form private sellers use, with the dealer's name on it: year, make, model, price, mileage, condition and place. Every vehicle gets its own page, a price rating, and a spot in search next to private sellers.

When a car is published, a short message confirms it's listed, and the inventory is up to date.

To take a car down, delete asks first: the listing will disappear from vehicle search. If the delete fails, you can simply try again.

## Verified financing professionals

Now the financing side. This is Marcus Chen, an independent financing professional in Mississauga. The green verified tag means his FSRA licence was checked by Wayfold.

His page lists the public questions he has answered, like whether the Civic financing offer is a good deal. And that answer appears on the Civic's own page, where buyers see it.

## Things to remember

- One free profile works for every kind of auto business.
- Dealers add, list and delete inventory, and each car gets a page and a price rating.
- Financing professionals are verified only after Wayfold checks their licence.
- Their public answers show on their profile and on the vehicle page.
