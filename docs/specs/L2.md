# L2 - Detailed Requirements

## L2-001: Registration
**Traces to:** L1-001

A visitor shall register with a display name, an email address and a password.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a visitor on the registration page, when they submit a display name, a valid unused email and a password of at least 12 characters, then an account is created and the user is signed in.
2. Given an email already registered, when a visitor submits it, then registration is rejected with a message that does not reveal whether the account exists beyond the form error shown.
3. Given a missing display name, an invalid email format or a password under 12 characters, when the form is submitted, then field-level errors are shown and no account is created.

---

## L2-002: Sign in and sign out
**Traces to:** L1-001

A registered user shall sign in with email and password and sign out at any time.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a registered user, when they submit correct credentials, then a session is created and they reach the home page.
2. Given incorrect credentials, when submitted, then sign-in fails with a generic error message.
3. Given a signed-in user, when they sign out, then the session is invalidated and protected pages redirect to sign-in.

---

## L2-003: Account management
**Traces to:** L1-001

A signed-in user shall be able to change their display name and password.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a signed-in user, when they save a new display name, then the new name is shown across the application.
2. Given a signed-in user, when they change the password with the correct current password, then the change succeeds and other sessions are invalidated.
3. Given an incorrect current password, when a change is submitted, then it is rejected.

---

## L2-006: Create a vehicle listing
**Traces to:** L1-003

A signed-in member shall create a private vehicle listing with title, description, price, vehicle details, photos, and GTA location, either from scratch or from a garage car.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a signed-in user, when they submit a listing with all required fields, then it is published and visible in browse results.
2. Given a missing required field or a negative price, when submitted, then field-level errors are shown and nothing is saved.
3. Given an uploaded file that is not an accepted image type or exceeds 10 MB, when uploaded, then it is rejected.
4. Given a signed-out visitor, when they attempt to create a listing, then they are redirected to sign-in.
5. Given a member creating a listing from a garage car, when the form opens, then the car's year, make, model and photo are pre-filled and remain editable.

---

## L2-007: Edit and remove listings
**Traces to:** L1-003

Only a listing's owner shall edit or remove it.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given the owner of a listing, when they edit it, then changes are saved and shown.
2. Given the owner, when they remove it, then it no longer appears in browse or search.
3. Given a different user, when they attempt to edit or remove it, then the request is rejected with 403.

---

## L2-008: Browse and search listings
**Traces to:** L1-003

Visitors and members shall browse private and dealer vehicle listings together and search by keyword, price range, make, model and seller type.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given published listings, when a user opens the marketplace, then listings are shown newest first, 20 per page.
2. Given a keyword, when searched, then only listings matching the keyword are shown.
3. Given a price range, when applied, then only listings within the inclusive range are shown.
4. Given a search with no matches, when it runs, then an empty-state message is shown.
5. Given a seller type filter of Private or Dealer, when applied, then only listings of that seller type are shown.

---

## L2-009: Inquire about a listing
**Traces to:** L1-003

A signed-in user shall send an inquiry message to a listing's owner.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a signed-in user viewing another user's listing, when they send a message, then the owner receives it.
2. Given the user's own listing, when viewed by the owner, then no inquiry action is offered.
3. Given a message over 2000 characters or empty, when sent, then it is rejected.

---

## L2-010: User profile
**Traces to:** L1-004

Each member shall have a profile page showing display name, optional bio, GTA area, garage, active listings, recommendations and posts.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a signed-in user, when they edit their bio (max 500 characters), then the profile shows it.
2. Given any signed-in user, when they open another member's profile, then name, bio, GTA area, garage, listings, recommendations and posts are shown in their respective sections.
3. Given a bio over 500 characters, when saved, then it is rejected.

---

## L2-011: Follow users
**Traces to:** L1-004

A signed-in user shall follow and unfollow other users.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a signed-in user on another's profile, when they follow, then the profile shows "Following" and the follower count increases by 1.
2. Given a followed user, when unfollowed, then the count decreases by 1.
3. Given a user's own profile, when viewed, then no follow action is offered.

---

## L2-012: Posts and feed
**Traces to:** L1-004

A signed-in member shall publish auto-related text posts with an auto topic of My car, Question, Recommendation or For sale and see a feed of posts from members they follow and themselves.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a signed-in user, when they publish a post of 1 to 1000 characters, then it appears at the top of their feed.
2. Given an empty or over-length post, when submitted, then it is rejected.
3. Given a followed user's post, when the feed loads, then it appears in reverse chronological order.
4. Given a post's author, when they delete it, then it disappears from all feeds.
5. Given post text containing HTML or script, when displayed, then it is rendered as escaped text.
6. Given a post without one of the four auto topics, when submitted, then it is rejected and not published.

---

## L2-013: Interact with posts
**Traces to:** L1-004

A signed-in user shall like and comment on posts.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a post, when a user likes it, then the like count increases by 1; liking again removes the like.
2. Given a post, when a user submits a comment of 1 to 500 characters, then it appears under the post.
3. Given an over-length comment, when submitted, then it is rejected.

---

## L2-014: GTA location scoping
**Traces to:** L1-005

Listings, providers and recommended products shall carry a location chosen from a defined list of Toronto and GTA municipalities, and users shall filter each directory by it.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given the listing form, when the location field is opened, then only Toronto and GTA municipalities are offered.
2. Given listings in several municipalities, when a user filters by one, then only listings in it are shown.
3. Given a location outside the list submitted directly to the API, when processed, then it is rejected.
4. Given providers or products in several municipalities, when a visitor filters a directory by one, then only results in that municipality are shown.

---

## L2-015: Responsive layout
**Traces to:** L1-006

All screens shall adapt to the five viewport breakpoints without horizontal scrolling.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given viewport widths of 360, 576, 768, 992, and 1200 px, when any page is viewed, then no horizontal scrollbar appears and all content is reachable.
2. Given viewport width <576px, when the marketplace is viewed, then listings display in a single column.
3. Given viewport width >=992px, when the marketplace is viewed, then listings display in multiple columns.
4. Given viewport width <768px, when navigation is viewed, then it collapses into a menu control operable by keyboard and touch.

---

## L2-016: WCAG 2.2 AA conformance
**Traces to:** L1-007

All screens shall meet WCAG 2.2 AA.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given any page, when scanned with an automated accessibility checker, then no AA violations are reported.
2. Given any interactive control, when the user navigates by keyboard only, then it is reachable, operable, and shows a visible focus indicator.
3. Given a form error, when it appears, then it is associated with its field and announced to assistive technology.
4. Given text and interactive components, when measured, then contrast meets AA minimums (4.5:1 text, 3:1 UI components).

---

## L2-017: Credential protection
**Traces to:** L1-008

Credentials and sessions shall be protected.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a stored account, when the database is inspected, then passwords are stored only as salted adaptive hashes.
2. Given 5 failed sign-ins for an account within 15 minutes, when a sixth is attempted, then it is blocked with HTTP 429 until the window ends.
3. Given any request, when served, then it uses HTTPS and session cookies are HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite.

---

## L2-018: Authorization and input validation
**Traces to:** L1-008

The server shall enforce authorization and validate all input.

**Status:** Basic checks are in place. Hardening is deferred until all features are complete and working (see AGENTS.md).

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a request without a valid session to a protected endpoint, when sent, then the response is 401.
2. Given a user acting on another user's resource, when sent, then the response is 403.
3. Given input containing SQL or script payloads, when sent, then it is not executed and is stored or displayed safely.
4. Given a state-changing request without a valid CSRF defense, when sent, then it is rejected.

---

## L2-019: Personal data protection
**Traces to:** L1-008

Personal data shall be minimized and removable.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a user's email address, when another user views their profile or listings, then the email is not shown.
2. Given a signed-in user, when they delete their account, then their profile, posts, and listings are removed.
3. Given logs, when inspected, then they contain no passwords or session tokens.

---

## L2-020: Response times
**Traces to:** L1-009

Pages and APIs shall respond quickly.

**Status:** Deferred until all features are complete and working (see AGENTS.md).

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given normal load, when API read requests are measured, then the 95th percentile is under 300 ms.
2. Given a listing browse page on a simulated mid-range mobile device over a 4G connection, when loaded, then Largest Contentful Paint is under 2.5 s.

---

## L2-021: Observability and health
**Traces to:** L1-009

The system shall expose health and emit structured logs.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a running system, when the health endpoint is requested, then it returns 200 when dependencies are available and 503 otherwise.
2. Given any server error, when it occurs, then a structured log entry with a request ID is written.
3. Given an unexpected error, when shown to a user, then the message is generic and contains no stack trace.
4. Given a request for a page that does not exist, when it is served, then a not-found page with a generic message and a link home is shown with HTTP 404.

---

## L2-022: Home church on profile
**Traces to:** L1-002

A member shall optionally enter a home church of up to 80 characters and choose whether signed-in members can see it.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a signed-in member, when they save a church name of at most 80 characters and enable Show my home church, then it appears on their profile to signed-in members.
2. Given a member who disables Show my home church, when another member views their profile, then the church is absent.
3. Given a church name longer than 80 characters, when saved, then the field shows an error and the value is not saved.
4. Given a signed-out visitor, when they request a member profile or its data, then the church name is not returned.

---

## L2-023: Church where members deal
**Traces to:** L1-002

An opted-in church affiliation shall appear with a private seller, recommendation author or provider owner only to signed-in members.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given an opted-in member, when another signed-in member views their listing seller panel or recommendation, then the home church appears with a cross icon and text.
2. Given a provider owner who opts in, when a signed-in member views the provider profile, then it states that the owner attends the church.
3. Given a signed-out visitor or an owner who has not opted in, when the same content loads, then no church affiliation is returned or displayed.

---

## L2-024: Garage cars
**Traces to:** L1-010

A member shall add, edit and remove cars in their garage with year, make, model, optional nickname, photo and notes.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a signed-in member, when they save a car with year, make and model, then it appears in their garage.
2. Given the owner, when they edit or remove a garage car, then the garage reflects the change.
3. Given another member, when they try to edit or remove the car, then the request is rejected with 403.
4. Given missing required fields or an invalid photo, when saved, then inline errors appear and no car is added.
5. Given a 360 px viewport, when the garage is shown, then cards fit in one column without horizontal scrolling.

---

## L2-025: Sell a garage car
**Traces to:** L1-010

A member shall start a private sale from a garage car.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given the owner of a garage car, when they choose Sell this car, then the listing form opens with year, make, model and photo pre-filled and editable.
2. Given another member, when they attempt to start a listing from that car, then the request is rejected with 403.
3. Given a saved listing, when the owner returns to the garage, then the car and its sale listing remain linked.

---

## L2-026: Recommend a provider
**Traces to:** L1-011

A signed-in member shall write one recommendation of 10 to 500 characters per provider; submitting another edits the existing recommendation.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a signed-in member and provider, when the member submits 10 to 500 characters, then the recommendation is shown with their name.
2. Given an existing recommendation from that member for the provider, when they submit a revision, then the existing entry changes and the count stays the same.
3. Given fewer than 10 or more than 500 characters, when submitted, then an inline error appears and nothing changes.
4. Given a signed-out visitor, when they select Recommend, then they are sent to sign-in.

---

## L2-027: Recommend an auto product
**Traces to:** L1-011

A signed-in member shall recommend an auto product by name, category, where they bought it and why they use it. Products shall not be sold on Wayfold.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a signed-in member, when they provide all four fields and submit, then the product recommendation appears with their name and purchase source.
2. Given an existing recommendation by that member for the same product, when they submit another, then it edits the first and the count stays the same.
3. Given a missing field, when submitted, then an inline error appears and no recommendation is saved.
4. Given a product page, when viewed at 360 px, then its content fits without horizontal scrolling and offers no purchase or checkout action.

---

## L2-028: Recommendation display
**Traces to:** L1-011

Written recommendations shall appear on the author's member profile, the provider page and the feed; provider and product pages shall show Recommended by N members without star ratings.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a published provider recommendation, when the author profile, provider page and signed-in feed load, then its author and text appear in each place.
2. Given recommendations by three distinct members, when the provider or product page loads, then it says Recommended by 3 members.
3. Given any recommendation surface, when viewed, then it shows no star rating.

---

## L2-029: Product discovery
**Traces to:** L1-011

Visitors shall browse recommended auto products by category and GTA municipality, sorted by number of recommending members.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given products with different recommendation counts, when the directory opens, then products with more recommending members appear first.
2. Given a selected category and municipality, when filters apply, then only matching products appear.
3. Given no matches, when filters apply, then a no-results message and a way to clear filters appear.

---

## L2-030: Provider profile
**Traces to:** L1-012

A provider profile shall show its category, GTA area, description, contact information and written recommendations. Categories shall be dealer, mechanic, body shop, tire shop, detailer, towing, insurance broker, financing professional or parts store.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a published provider, when its page opens, then its name, category, area, description, contact and recommendations appear.
2. Given a category outside the defined list, when submitted, then it is rejected.
3. Given a 360 px viewport, when the profile opens, then all content and actions remain reachable without horizontal scrolling.

---

## L2-031: Provider directory
**Traces to:** L1-012

Visitors shall find providers by category, keyword and GTA municipality, ordered by distinct member recommendation count.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given published providers, when the directory opens, then the most recommended providers appear first with their counts.
2. Given a category, keyword or municipality filter, when applied, then only matching providers appear.
3. Given no matches, when filters apply, then a no-results message and a way to clear filters appear.

---

## L2-032: Manage a provider profile
**Traces to:** L1-012

A signed-in member shall create and manage a provider profile for their business.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a signed-in member, when they submit a name, category, GTA municipality, description and contact information, then a provider profile is created under their ownership.
2. Given the owner, when they edit their profile, then changes appear publicly.
3. Given a different member, when they attempt to edit it, then the request is rejected with 403.
4. Given missing required information, when submitted, then inline errors appear and no profile is created.

---

## L2-033: Financing credential verification
**Traces to:** L1-012

Financing professionals shall submit a licence or credential. A Verified badge shall appear only after Wayfold checks it.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a financing professional creating a profile, when they omit a credential, then the form rejects submission.
2. Given a submitted but unchecked credential, when the profile appears, then no Verified badge is shown.
3. Given a credential marked verified by Wayfold, when the profile appears, then the Verified badge is shown.

---

## L2-034: Dealer inventory
**Traces to:** L1-003

Dealer providers shall list inventory through the vehicle listing form, and each dealer listing shall link to its dealer profile.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given the owner of a dealer provider, when they publish a valid vehicle listing, then it appears in the dealer inventory and the shared vehicle search.
2. Given a dealer listing, when its card or detail page appears, then the dealer name links to its profile.
3. Given a non-owner or a provider of another category, when they try to add dealer inventory, then the request is rejected with 403.

---

## L2-035: Vehicle detail
**Traces to:** L1-003

Every vehicle shall have a detail page with year, make, model, price, photos, description, mileage, condition, features, GTA area and a seller panel for either a private member or dealer.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a published private listing, when its detail page opens, then all vehicle fields and the member seller panel appear.
2. Given a published dealer listing, when its detail page opens, then all vehicle fields and a dealer profile link appear.
3. Given a 360 px viewport, when either listing opens, then photos, details and contact action are reachable without horizontal scrolling.

---

## L2-036: Automatic price rating
**Traces to:** L1-013

A vehicle shall show Great, Good, Fair or High based on at least five comparable GTA listings; with fewer than five, no rating shall appear.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given at least five comparable GTA listings, when a vehicle detail page loads, then one of Great, Good, Fair or High appears with the comparison context.
2. Given fewer than five comparable GTA listings, when the page loads, then no price rating appears and the page explains why.
3. Given a signed-out visitor, when the page loads, then the same rating or no-rating result is visible.

---

## L2-037: Ask about a deal
**Traces to:** L1-013

Signed-in members shall ask public deal questions of 1 to 1000 characters with a topic.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a signed-in member, when they submit a topic and a question of 1 to 1000 characters, then the question appears publicly on the vehicle page.
2. Given a missing topic, empty question or question over 1000 characters, when submitted, then an inline error appears and nothing is posted.
3. Given a signed-out visitor, when they try to ask, then they are sent to sign-in.

---

## L2-038: Expert answers
**Traces to:** L1-013

Only verified financing professionals shall answer deal questions; their answers shall show the badge and notify the asker.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a verified financing professional, when they answer a public question, then the answer appears with their name and Verified badge and the asker is notified.
2. Given an unverified provider or ordinary member, when they attempt to answer, then the request is rejected with 403.
3. Given a visitor, when they view the question, then its public answer is visible without a church affiliation.

---

## L2-039: Visitor access
**Traces to:** L1-004

Signed-out visitors shall browse vehicles, providers and products. The feed and member profiles shall require sign-in, and church affiliation shall never be returned to visitors.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a signed-out visitor, when they open vehicle, provider or product pages, then public content appears without church affiliation.
2. Given a signed-out visitor, when they request the feed or a member profile, then they are redirected to sign-in and no protected profile data is returned.
3. Given a signed-in member, when they open the feed or a member profile, then permitted content appears.
