# L1 - High-Level Requirements

## L1-001: Accounts and identity
Visitors must be able to register, sign in, and manage an account.

## L1-002: Christian identity as a trust signal
Members can optionally show their home church on their profile and see which members and providers attend a church. Anyone in the GTA can join. Wayfold has no church content or church administration.

## L1-003: Automotive marketplace
Members must be able to list private sales, and dealers must be able to list inventory. Every vehicle must have a detail page, and all vehicles must be searchable together.

## L1-004: Auto community
Members must be able to create profiles, follow one another, post about cars, and like and comment on posts in an autos-only community.

## L1-005: Toronto / GTA focus
Content, listings, and discovery must be oriented to Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area. Users must be able to filter or scope by location within the GTA.

## L1-006: Responsive experience
Every screen must work on extra small (<576px), small (>=576px), medium (>=768px), large (>=992px), and extra large (>=1200px) viewports.

## L1-007: Accessibility
The application must meet WCAG 2.2 AA.

## L1-008: Security and privacy
The system must protect authentication, authorization, input handling, and personal data, and must address the OWASP Top 10.

## L1-009: Performance and reliability
The system must respond quickly under expected load. It must also be observable and operable in production.

## L1-010: Garage
Members must be able to share the cars they own.

## L1-011: Recommendations
Members must be able to recommend providers and auto products, and see who recommends what. Products are not sold on Wayfold.

## L1-012: Provider profiles
Auto industry providers of every kind must have searchable profiles managed by their owners.

## L1-013: Deal guidance
Vehicles must show an automatic price rating when comparable listings are available, and verified financing professionals must be able to answer public questions about deals.
